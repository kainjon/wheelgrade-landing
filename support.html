Frequently asked questions

What does WheelGrade do?

It grades an options wheel trade (a cash-secured put or a covered call) A through F before you make it, then tracks every dollar of premium you collect.

What tickers does it cover?

The Analyzer grades any ticker, but the Elite 32 are the only names the app treats as verified A+ quality. Off-list names are capped at a C grade.

How much does it cost?

$9.99 one-time. Every feature, every platform, no subscription.

How do I add a trade to my portfolio?

Grade it in the Analyzer and tap ‘Add to my portfolio,’ or add a trade you’ve already made from the Portfolio tab.

Does it connect to my brokerage?

No. WheelGrade does not connect to any brokerage and cannot see your account. You enter your trades yourself (about 30 seconds per trade), and the app tracks them from there.

What if I lose my phone or switch devices?

Your portfolio is tied to your account (created at purchase), so it follows you. Sign in on the new device and your portfolio is there.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds go through the store you bought it from (Apple App Store or Google Play), under their refund policy. For a web purchase, email support@wheelgrade.com.
