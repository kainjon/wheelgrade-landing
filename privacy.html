WheelGrade Privacy Policy

In plain English: WheelGrade collects only what it needs to grade your trades and show your portfolio — and it doesn't sell or share that data. Here's exactly what that means.

Last updated: November 13, 2026 Applies to the WheelGrade app and wheelgrade.com

At a glance

  • Your trades stay yours. We don't sell, rent, or share your portfolio data.
  • No personal data goes to market-data providers. Prices are public; your information isn't sent with them.
  • You can delete. Remove a position, your whole portfolio, or your account from inside the app.
1

What WheelGrade is

WheelGrade is a phone app that grades options wheel trades — cash-secured puts and covered calls — and tracks the premium you collect.

You enter a trade you're considering or have already made, the app grades it, and your Portfolio keeps a running total of your premium, including lifetime premium by ticker.

2

Information we collect

  • The trades and positions you enter Tickers, strikes, expirations, premiums, assignments, and call-aways. This is your portfolio data, stored so the app can show your totals and lifetime premium.
  • Account information — once you create an account Accounts are created at purchase time. When you create one, we store your sign-in identity (Apple or Google) and your purchase status, so your portfolio can be tied to you and follow you across devices.
  • Market data The app fetches public stock prices — live when you open it — and delayed option quotes (15 minutes delayed) to grade trades and refresh the board. No personal information is sent to the market-data providers.
Before you create an account: the app stores your portfolio only on your device / in your browser. It isn't tied to an account yet.
3

How we use it

Only to run the app for you — to grade your trades, show your portfolio and premium totals, and show the Elite 32 board.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or your portfolio data with anyone.

4

Data storage and security

Once accounts exist, your portfolio is tied to your account and is only visible to you.

The app owner's own portfolio is stored separately and is never visible to other users.

5

Your choices

You can delete a position or your whole portfolio from inside the app at any time.

Deleting your account deletes your portfolio data.

6

Children's privacy

WheelGrade is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change, and material changes will be noted in the app.

8
