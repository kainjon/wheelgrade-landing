02 — Analyzer

Grade the trade before you make it

Type a ticker, pick a covered call or cash-secured put, pick an expiration. WheelGrade pulls the live option chain and grades the trade A through F — on quality, rent, delta, the earnings print gate, basis if assigned, and liquidity. Verdict up top, numbers underneath, and the one or two things to change.

  • Quality first

    The grade starts with the company, not the premium. Anything outside the Elite 32 is capped — a fat premium on a weak name never earns an A.

  • The 1% rent gate

    Premium is measured as rent on your capital, per month. Below the gate, the trade has to earn its way back in — or it gets marked down.

  • The print gate is a hard fail

    Earnings inside the expiration? The grade stops cold. No exceptions, even for the best names on the list.

  • ~30 delta, checked

    The suggested strike targets roughly 30 delta — the wheel's working range — and the grade tells you when your strike drifts too hot or too cold.

  • Basis if assigned

    Every put grade shows the price you'd actually own the stock at after premium — the number that decides whether assignment is a win or a trap.

  • Liquidity

    Thin chains and wide spreads get flagged, so the premium on screen is premium you can actually collect.

04 — The Wheel

How the wheel strategy works

The wheel is an options income strategy with two moving parts. Sell a cash-secured put on a stock you'd be glad to own. If it expires worthless, keep the premium and sell another. If you're assigned, you own the shares at a discount to where they traded — then sell covered calls above your cost until the shares are called away, and the wheel turns again. Every step is a decision about quality and price, and every step can be graded before you make it.

  • Sell the put

    Around 30 delta on a quality name, rent at or above 1% a month, and never into an earnings print. How the cash-secured put grade works

  • Own it at your basis

    Assignment isn't a loss — it's buying the stock at the strike minus the premium you kept. The grade shows that basis before you commit a dollar.

  • Sell the call

    Covered calls at or above your basis, rent first. If the shares are called away, that profit is the plan working — not a mistake. How the covered call grade works

  • Repeat — on quality only

    The wheel only works on stocks worth owning through a drawdown. The Elite 32 is the roster; the Analyzer grades every turn. The wheel strategy calculator
